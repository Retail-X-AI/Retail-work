Retail-X-AI — Project Scope and Objectives

1. Project Title

**Retail-X-AI: AI-Powered Inventory Demand Forecasting and Smart Restocking System for Local Spaza Shops**

2. Project Overview

Retail-X-AI is an AI-powered retail inventory management solution designed to support local spaza shops and small grocery retailers.

The system will use sales and inventory data to analyse product performance, identify sales trends, forecast future product demand, detect possible stockouts, and provide smart restocking recommendations.

The solution is intended to help small retailers make better inventory decisions using data and AI rather than relying only on manual estimation and experience.

3. Problem Statement

Local spaza shops and small grocery retailers may experience difficulties with:

* Identifying products that sell quickly or slowly.
* Knowing when stock needs to be replenished.
* Determining appropriate reorder quantities.
* Predicting future customer demand.
* Detecting products that may run out of stock.
* Monitoring sales trends across products and stores.
* Making purchasing decisions using limited or unstructured information.

These challenges can contribute to stockouts, overstocking, poor purchasing decisions and reduced visibility of business performance.

4. Proposed Solution

Retail-X-AI will provide an integrated AI and data-driven solution that analyses retail sales and inventory information.

The system will provide functionality for:

1. Sales and inventory data analysis.
2. Product and store performance analysis.
3. Demand forecasting.
4. Stockout risk identification.
5. Smart reorder recommendations.
6. Identification of slow-moving products.
7. Inventory monitoring.
8. AI-assisted retail queries through a chatbot.
9. Dashboard-based presentation of important retail information.

The system will use Python-based data analysis and AI/ML components together with a database and application interface.

 5. Project Objectives

The main objectives of Retail-X-AI are to:

* Analyse historical retail sales and inventory data.
* Identify product sales patterns and trends.
* Forecast future product demand using machine learning techniques.
* Identify products that may experience stock shortages.
* Recommend suitable reorder quantities based on available information.
* Identify slow-moving products that may require attention.
* Provide retailers with accessible business and inventory insights.
* Develop an AI-assisted chatbot capable of answering relevant retail inventory questions.
* Present important information through a practical dashboard.
* Demonstrate how AI and data analytics can support inventory decisions in small retail businesses.

6. Project Scope

6.1 In Scope

The project will include:

* Retail sales data preparation and cleaning.
* Inventory-related data analysis.
* Product performance analysis.
* Store performance analysis.
* Sales trend and seasonality analysis.
* Demand forecasting.
* Forecast model evaluation.
* Stockout prediction or risk identification.
* Smart restocking and reorder recommendations.
* Slow-moving product analysis.
* Database design and implementation.
* AI/ML processing using Python.
* AI-assisted retail chatbot functionality.
* Dashboard and system integration.
* Testing of the developed solution.
* Deployment documentation.

 6.2 Out of Scope

The following are outside the initial project scope:

* Direct integration with commercial banking systems.
* Real-time supplier purchasing and payment processing.
* Automated physical stock counting using specialised hardware.
* Full enterprise resource planning functionality.
* Large-scale multi-national retail management.
* Autonomous purchasing without retailer confirmation.
* Guaranteed prediction of future customer behaviour.

 7. Target Users

The primary target users are:

* Local spaza shop owners.
* Small grocery shop owners.
* Retail store managers.
* Staff responsible for inventory and purchasing decisions.

8. Expected Benefits

Retail-X-AI is expected to provide users with:

* Better visibility of sales and inventory information.
* Earlier awareness of potential stock shortages.
* Data-supported reorder decisions.
* Improved understanding of product performance.
* Identification of slow-moving products.
* Easier access to retail insights through an AI assistant.
* A centralised view of important inventory and sales information.

9. Key Project Deliverables

The project is expected to produce:

* Business analysis documentation.
* Requirements documentation.
* System analysis and design documentation.
* Database design and implementation.
* Prepared retail dataset.
* Data analysis components.
* Demand forecasting models.
* Stockout and restocking components.
* AI chatbot component.
* Application/dashboard components.
* Testing evidence.
* Deployment documentation.
* Final integrated Retail-X-AI solution.

 10. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

* The approved project scope and objectives are documented.
* The system can process the selected retail dataset.
* Sales and inventory information can be analysed.
* A demand forecasting component is implemented and evaluated.
* Potential stockout situations can be identified.
* Restocking recommendations can be generated.
* Relevant retail information can be presented through the application/dashboard.
* The AI assistant can respond to supported retail inventory questions.
* The major system components can be tested and demonstrated.
* Project work is documented and maintained in the GitHub repository.

 11. Project Boundary

Retail-X-AI is intended to provide **decision support** for small retailers. AI-generated forecasts and recommendations will assist the retailer but will not automatically make purchasing decisions on behalf of the business owner.

The quality of forecasts and recommendations will depend on the availability, quality and characteristics of the data used by the system.
